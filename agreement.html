Key rental terms

The renter agrees to pay the displayed weekly rental rate through recurring automatic billing while the rental remains active; maintain a valid driver's license; use the vehicle only for lawful approved purposes; comply with applicable rideshare and delivery-platform requirements; promptly report accidents, damage, citations, tolls and mechanical issues; follow maintenance instructions; not permit unauthorized drivers; and return the vehicle when required by the agreement.

GigReady does not guarantee approval by Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, Amazon Flex or any other platform. Platform eligibility depends on current platform rules, the specific vehicle, insurance, driver status and local requirements.

Failed recurring payments, prohibited use, material misrepresentation, loss of eligibility or failure to return the vehicle may result in suspension or lawful recovery as allowed by the final rental agreement and applicable law.
