Questions before you start?

Does eligibility guarantee a car?

No. Prequalification helps determine fit. Final approval still depends on identity, license, driving history, insurance, documents, payment, and current vehicle availability.

How do weekly payments work?

Once your rental is activated, the weekly rate for your selected vehicle is billed automatically according to your rental agreement.

Can I use the car for Uber or Lyft?

Platform eligibility depends on the vehicle, driver account, current platform rules, local requirements, insurance, and the final rental arrangement.

What happens after I choose a car?

You securely verify your driver's license and identity, complete payment setup and final review, then schedule pickup. Bring your physical driver's license when you collect the vehicle.
